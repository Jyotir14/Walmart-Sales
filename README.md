# Walmart Sales

## Objective:
The Walmart Sales Data Analysis project aims to explore Walmart's sales data to uncover key trends, seasonality, and patterns in sales performance. It focuses on analyzing various aspects of the sales data to improve sales strategies and optimize performance across different branches and product lines.​
## Features:
## 1. Product Analysis:
Examines the performance of different product lines, identifying top-selling categories and product lines, and determining areas for improvement.
## 2. Sales Analysis:
Analyzes sales trends, customer types, and sales per time of day. This helps in evaluating the effectiveness of sales strategies and identifying necessary adjustments​.
## 3.Customer Analysis: 
Segments customers based on their purchasing behavior, gender, and preferred payment methods. It helps in understanding the most profitable customer segments.
## 4.Time-Based Features:
The project includes feature engineering, such as adding columns like "time_of_day," "day_name," and "month_name" to better understand the impact of time on sales performance.
# Purpose:
The primary purpose of the Walmart Sales Data Analysis project is to gain valuable insights into Walmart's sales transactions, identify high-performing branches and products, and understand customer behaviors. The analysis helps to optimize sales strategies by exploring various factors influencing sales across different branches and product lines​.
By addressing critical business questions, this project aims to support data-driven decision-making to enhance sales performance and overall business efficiency.
